Connect your Last.fm account so YTMDesktop2 can update **Now Playing** and scrobble tracks that meet Last.fm’s listening rules (half the track duration or four minutes, whichever is shorter; tracks under 30 seconds are skipped).

![Last.fm settings](/images/feature-lastfm.jpg)

## Setup

1. Open **Settings → Last.fm**.
2. Authorize the app with your Last.fm account.
3. Keep the app running while you listen — scrobbles settle after the track threshold.

![Last.fm connected](/images/feature-lastfm2.jpg)

## Notes

- Switching tracks mid-listen abandons a pending scrobble unless the threshold was already crossed.
- Last.fm updates are debounced so rapid skipping does not spam the API.
- Auth tokens are kept in an [encrypted store](/docs/internals/encrypted-stores) — clearing app data or the key file requires re-auth.
- Disconnect or toggle Last.fm anytime from the same settings page.
