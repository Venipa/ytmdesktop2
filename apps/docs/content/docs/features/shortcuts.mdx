---
title: Global shortcuts
description: Custom OS-wide media keybinds in Settings
---

YTMDesktop2 can register **global** shortcuts so media controls work even when another app is focused.

Defaults:

- Previous track: `Shift+Alt+Left`
- Next track: `Shift+Alt+Right`
- Play / pause: `Shift+Alt+Space`

Extra actions (play, pause, seek 10s, volume, like, dislike, shuffle, repeat) are listed in settings and stay unbound until you set them.

## Setup

1. Open **Settings > Player > Shortcuts**.
2. Leave **Enable global shortcuts** on (off unregisters every bind).
3. Click a bind, then press a combo. Escape cancels. Backspace or Delete clears.
4. Use **Reset** on a row to restore that action's default, or **Reset all** (with confirm) to restore the three defaults and unbind the rest.

## Safety

Binds are checked in the main process before they are saved:

- Must include Ctrl, Alt, or Cmd (Shift alone is rejected so typing is not stolen).
- OS-reserved combos (for example `Alt+F4`, `Alt+Tab`, `Win+L`, `Cmd+Q`) are blocked.
- Media/volume keys stay with the system media session.
- Duplicates across actions are rejected.
- Electron also probes `globalShortcut.register`. If the OS or another app already owns the combo, the change is refused.

While you are capturing a bind, registered shortcuts are temporarily unregistered so the old combo does not fire.
