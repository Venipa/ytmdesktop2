<Callout type="warn" title="Removed in 1.4.0">
  The mini player was removed in **1.4.0**. Use [tray view](/docs/features/tray-view) instead — click the system tray / menu bar icon for compact now-playing controls.
</Callout>

The mini player was a small always-on-top window for quick controls and now-playing info when you did not need the full desktop UI.

![Mini player](/images/feature-miniplayer.jpg)

## Former setup

1. Open the mini player from the app (tray / window controls, depending on your build).
2. Keep it above other windows while you work.
3. Use transport controls and track info without restoring the main window.

## Tips (historical)

- Pair with Discord or Last.fm — those integrations keep running while the mini player is open.
- Collapse back to the full player when you need the queue, lyrics, or library.
- Cover art used the [thumbnail cache](/docs/internals/thumbnail-cache) (`ytmd-thumb://`), same as [tray view](/docs/features/tray-view).
